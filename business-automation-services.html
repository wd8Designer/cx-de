Business Process Automation (RPA)

RPA Services for Rule-Based Tasks in Systems Without APIs

Some systems can only be operated through their screens, so staff copy data between them by hand. Our robotic process automation services build bots that perform these steps, logging every run and sending anything outside the rules to an exception queue. We also tell you when RPA is the wrong fit and an API integration would last longer.

  • ✓ Process Assessment: Checks whether a task is stable and rule-based enough for RPA.
  • ✓ Unattended Bots: Run scheduled tasks such as nightly reconciliations without staff involvement.
  • ✓ Attended Bots: Assist staff at their desks by completing repetitive steps on request.
  • ✓ Document Processing: Extracts data from invoices, forms, and emails for bots to use.
  • ✓ Exception Queues: Send unusual cases to staff with the reason and the data involved.
  • ✓ Run Logs: Record every bot action for troubleshooting and audit review.
Workflow Automation

Workflow Automation Services That Connect Systems and People

Many processes stall in the gaps between systems: a form waits for approval, data is re-entered in another tool, or nobody notices a failed step. Our workflow automation services connect those systems through APIs, add approval steps where people must decide, and route errors to the right owner. If a step needs judgment, we can bring in an AI agent.

  • ✓ Event Triggers: Start workflows from form submissions, record changes, or scheduled times.
  • ✓ API Integrations: Move data between your ERP, CRM, finance, and support tools.
  • ✓ Approval Steps: Pause workflows until the right person approves, with reminders and escalations.
  • ✓ Error Routing: Send failed steps to a named owner with details to fix them.
  • ✓ Retry Logic: Repeat temporary failures automatically before alerting your team.
  • ✓ Workflow Monitoring: Show every run, its status, and processing time in one view.
Marketing & CRM Automation

Marketing and CRM Automation Services Built on Clean Data

Automated campaigns and sales follow-ups fail quietly when CRM records are incomplete or duplicated. Our marketing automation services and CRM automation services set up lead scoring, routing, and lifecycle emails, while data quality rules stop duplicates and missing fields from spreading. Sync between your CRM and other systems keeps every team working from the same record.

  • ✓ Lead Scoring: Ranks leads by fit and engagement so sales prioritizes the right ones.
  • ✓ Lead Routing: Assigns leads to the right owner by region, product, or deal size.
  • ✓ Lifecycle Emails: Sends onboarding, nurture, and renewal messages triggered by customer actions.
  • ✓ Data Quality Rules: Merges duplicates, fills required fields, and flags incomplete records.
  • ✓ CRM and ERP Sync: Keeps contacts, deals, and orders consistent across systems.
  • ✓ Consent Management: Respects opt-in status and unsubscribe requests in every campaign.
Operational Reliability

Automation built for reliability, visibility, and control

An automation that fails without anyone noticing can do more damage than the manual process it replaced. These four practices are part of every intelligent automation service we deliver.

  • ✓ Exception Handling: Every automation has defined paths for missing data, unexpected screens, and system outages, with cases routed to staff.
  • ✓ Run Monitoring and Alerts: Dashboards show each run's status, and alerts reach the right owner when a run fails or slows down.
  • ✓ Access and Audit Logs: Bots and workflows use their own credentials with limited rights, and every action is logged for review.
  • ✓ Documented Maintenance: Each automation is documented and versioned, so changes in connected systems can be handled quickly by your team or ours.

Discuss Your Processes

Which processes are best suited to RPA?

RPA suits stable, high-volume tasks with clear rules and structured inputs, especially in systems without an API, such as copying data between legacy applications or downloading reports. Processes that change often or need judgment are better served by workflow automation or an AI agent.

What is the difference between RPA, workflow automation, and AI agents?

RPA operates software through its screens, step by step. Workflow automation connects systems through APIs and coordinates steps between them and people. AI agents decide their next step based on a goal, which suits tasks that rules cannot fully describe.

What happens when an automation fails?

The failed case moves to an exception queue or owner with the reason and the data involved, and an alert is sent. Temporary failures are retried automatically first. Every run is logged, so the cause can be traced and fixed.

Which automation platforms do you work with?

We work with widely used RPA, workflow, and CRM platforms, including UiPath, Microsoft Power Automate, n8n, Make, HubSpot, and Salesforce. Where possible, we build on platforms you already license, so automations fit your existing stack and support arrangements.

How do you keep CRM data consistent across systems?

We define which system owns each field, sync changes through APIs on a set schedule or in real time, and apply rules that merge duplicates and flag incomplete records. Sync errors are logged and routed to an owner for correction.

Is marketing automation compatible with GDPR?

It can be, if consent is handled correctly. We configure automations to respect opt-in status, honor unsubscribe requests immediately, and limit data to what each campaign needs. Your data protection officer or legal team confirms the lawful basis for processing.

Who maintains the automations after launch?

You can choose. We offer ongoing monitoring and maintenance, or we hand over documentation, credentials, and runbooks so your team can manage the automations. Either way, changes in connected systems are the most common reason updates are needed.

How much do business process automation services cost?

Cost depends on the number and complexity of processes, how many systems are involved, exception handling needs, platform licensing, and whether you want ongoing maintenance. We provide a scoped estimate after process discovery and selection.
