Financial institutions are caught between legacy operational silos, escalating fraud attacks, and aggressive neo-bank competition. Here is how we bridge the divide.

CHALLENGE 01

Legacy Core Banking Entrapment

Traditional core ledgers (COBOL, older AS400, legacy SAP) are fragile, batch-oriented, and slow. Modifying them requires multi-year projects with unacceptable outage risks.

The Cypherox Engineering Solution: We implement the Strangler Fig pattern with event-driven Kafka middleware. New digital features deploy on agile microservices while asynchronously reconciling with your legacy ledger without operational risk.
CHALLENGE 02

Real-Time Instant Payment Fraud

With SEPA Instant and FedNow, funds settle in seconds. Legacy rule-based fraud detection causes either massive false-positive rejections or devastating unauthorized fraud losses.

The Cypherox Engineering Solution: We build sub-15ms AI anomaly pipelines utilizing Graph Neural Networks and behavioral biometrics. Fraudulent transfers are intercepted in-flight with an 84% reduction in false positive declines.
CHALLENGE 03

Escalating Regulatory Audit Burden

Compliance with BaFin, DORA, AMLD6, and ESG reporting consumes millions in manual analyst hours and risks severe regulatory fines for inaccurate disclosures.

The Cypherox Engineering Solution: Automated RegTech reporting engines. Continuous transaction monitoring parses logs in real-time, producing automated filings formatted strictly to European supervisory templates.
CHALLENGE 04

Open Banking & Disintermediation

PSD2 and upcoming PSD3 mandates force banks to share customer data. Without a monetization strategy, financial institutions become commoditized utility balance sheets.

The Cypherox Engineering Solution: We engineer revenue-generating Embedded Finance APIs. Transform your bank into a platform providing white-label lending, BaaS (Banking-as-a-Service), and commercial payroll rails.
