Navigating the High-Stakes Shifts in Financial Services

Financial institutions are caught between legacy operational silos, escalating fraud attacks, and aggressive neo-bank competition. Here is how we bridge the divide.

01

Legacy Core Banking Entrapment

The Industry Problem Traditional core ledgers (COBOL, AS400, older SAP) are fragile, batch-oriented, and slow. Modifying them requires multi-year projects with unacceptable outage risks.
The Cypherox Engineering Solution We implement the Strangler Fig pattern with event-driven Kafka middleware. New digital features deploy on agile microservices while asynchronously reconciling with your legacy ledger without risk.
02

Real-Time Instant Payment Fraud

The Industry Problem With SEPA Instant and FedNow, funds settle in seconds. Legacy rule-based fraud detection causes either massive false-positive rejections or devastating unauthorized fraud losses.
The Cypherox Engineering Solution We build sub-15ms AI anomaly pipelines utilizing Graph Neural Networks and behavioral biometrics. Fraudulent transfers are intercepted in-flight with an 84% reduction in false positive declines.
03

Escalating Regulatory Audit Burden

The Industry Problem Compliance with BaFin, DORA, AMLD6, and ESG reporting consumes millions in manual analyst hours and risks severe regulatory fines for inaccurate disclosures.
The Cypherox Engineering Solution Automated RegTech reporting engines. Continuous transaction monitoring parses logs in real-time, producing automated filings formatted strictly to European supervisory templates.
04

Open Banking & Disintermediation

The Industry Problem PSD2 and upcoming PSD3 mandates force banks to share customer data. Without a monetization strategy, financial institutions become commoditized utility balance sheets.
The Cypherox Engineering Solution We engineer revenue-generating Embedded Finance APIs. Transform your bank into a platform providing white-label lending, BaaS (Banking-as-a-Service), and commercial payroll rails.
