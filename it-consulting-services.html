How Cypherox Consults

How a consulting engagement moves to a decision

Good recommendations depend on understanding the business before the technology. Our five stages move from interviews and assessment to a roadmap you can approve and act on.

Context and Stakeholder Interviews

We speak with leadership, engineering, and operations to understand goals, constraints, budgets, and the decision that needs making.

Current-State Assessment

Options and Trade-Off Analysis

We develop realistic options and compare them on cost, effort, risk, and business impact.

Roadmap and Business Case

We turn the preferred option into a sequenced plan with budgets, milestones, and measurable outcomes.

Implementation Support

Frequently Asked Questions

Answers for CTOs, founders, and executives considering IT consulting services, covering deliverables, startup and enterprise differences, AI strategy, independence, and cost.

Discuss Your Decision

What do your IT consulting services deliver?

Each engagement ends with written recommendations: a current-state assessment, the options we considered, the trade-offs between them, and a sequenced roadmap with estimated costs and milestones. You can act on it with your own team, another partner, or Cypherox.

How is startup IT consulting different from IT consulting for established companies?

Startups need speed and focus: the right stack, a tight MVP, and architecture that will not block growth. Established companies usually need to change existing systems safely, which means more attention to integration, migration risk, and keeping operations running.

What does an AI readiness assessment cover?

It reviews whether your data, systems, skills, and governance can support the AI use cases you are considering. You learn which use cases are feasible now, which need preparation first, and what that preparation involves.

How do you prioritize AI use cases?

We score each use case on business value, technical feasibility, data availability, and regulatory risk. High-value, feasible ideas with available data move to the front of the roadmap, and we explain why others should wait or be dropped.

Is your advice independent of specific vendors?

Our recommendations are based on your requirements, budget, and team. We regularly compare commercial products, open-source options, and custom builds, and we will recommend an existing product when it serves you better than building one.

Do you give legal advice on the EU AI Act or GDPR?

No. We identify technical and governance points that may fall under the EU AI Act or GDPR, such as risk classification or data handling, and document them for your legal team or external counsel to confirm.

Can Cypherox implement the roadmap after consulting?

Yes, if you choose. Our teams deliver AI agents, generative AI systems, machine learning models, automation, and UI/UX design, so recommendations can move into delivery without a handover gap. You are free to use another partner instead.

How much do IT consulting services cost?

Cost depends on the scope of the question, the number of systems or use cases reviewed, stakeholder availability, and whether you need ongoing advisory support. Focused assessments can be priced as fixed engagements, and we confirm scope and cost before work starts.
