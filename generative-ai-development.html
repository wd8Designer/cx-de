How Cypherox Delivers GenAI

Our five-stage generative AI delivery lifecycle

Most generative AI problems start with unprepared content, not with the model. Our process audits your data first, then designs retrieval, tests outputs, and monitors quality after launch.

Discovery and Content Audit

We confirm the use case, review the documents and data the system will rely on, and agree how quality will be measured.

Architecture and Model Selection

We choose models, retrieval design, and hosting based on accuracy needs, data sensitivity, response time, and running cost.

Retrieval Setup and Tuning

We prepare, chunk, and index your content, then tune prompts and retrieval until answers meet the agreed quality bar.

Guardrails and Testing

We add output filters and fallback rules, then test against real questions, difficult cases, and attempts to misuse the system.

Deployment and Quality Monitoring

We release to users in stages and track answer quality, unanswered questions, latency, and cost, adjusting as content changes.

Discuss Your Use Case

What do generative AI development services include?

They cover use-case selection, preparing your content for retrieval, choosing models, building the application, adding guardrails, testing output quality, and monitoring after launch. The aim is a system that answers or drafts from your verified data, not a general-purpose demo.

What is the difference between generative AI and AI agents?

Generative AI produces answers, drafts, translations, and summaries for people to use. AI agents go further and take actions in your systems, such as updating records. If your use case needs the system to act, see our AI agent development services.

How does RAG reduce wrong answers?

Retrieval-augmented generation finds relevant passages in your documents and gives them to the model before it responds. The model answers from that material instead of relying on general training data, and each answer can cite its source for checking. Our RAG development services include tuning this retrieval step.

Will our data be used to train AI models?

No. We configure model access so your prompts, documents, and outputs are excluded from provider training, and we can use self-hosted models where your policies require it. Data handling terms are confirmed in writing before any project data is shared.

Where are generative AI systems hosted?

Hosting depends on data sensitivity and your GDPR obligations. Options include model providers' EU regions, your own cloud account, or self-hosted open-weight models. We settle hosting during architecture and model selection, because it affects which models are available.

How is an AI chatbot different from a virtual assistant?

A chatbot answers questions for customers or staff from approved content. A virtual assistant works for an individual user, drafting emails, summarizing meetings, and tracking follow-ups inside their own tools and with their own access rights.

Does the EU AI Act apply to generative AI systems?

Yes, in several ways. Chatbots must tell users they are interacting with AI, and some AI-generated content must be identifiable as such. We build these transparency measures into the system, while your legal team confirms which obligations apply to your use case.

How much do generative AI development services cost?

Cost depends on the capability, the volume and condition of your content, the number of integrations, quality and review requirements, and hosting choice. Model usage is an ongoing cost that grows with traffic. We provide a scoped estimate after the discovery and content audit.
