Ask our AI architects

How do your AI Chatbots prevent hallucinations and guarantee data accuracy?

We implement a multi-layered Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) architecture with strict semantic guardrails. Rather than relying on generic model memory, the chatbot answers exclusively based on retrieved chunks from your verified corporate knowledge base. If no high-confidence data exists, the model is programmed to decline to answer and gracefully hand off to a human agent, achieving 99.5%+ factual accuracy.

What distinguishes your Virtual Assistant Services from standard chatbots?

While chatbots primarily converse and retrieve text, our Virtual Assistants are autonomous agents endowed with secure function calling and tool use. They can take real-world actions: querying an ERP, initiating database updates, scheduling complex meetings across timezones, drafting personalized emails, and orchestrating multi-step business processes under strict role-based access controls.

How does your AI Language Translator handle industry-specific jargon and compliance?

We combine domain-adapted neural machine translation models (such as fine-tuned Llama 3 and MarianMT) with dynamic vector glossary enforcement. Technical terms, ISO compliance specifications, and trademarks are locked into deterministic terminology tables, preventing translation drift across 100+ supported languages.

How is our proprietary company data protected? Does it train external models?

Never. We sign strict Non-Disclosure Agreements (NDAs) and deploy solutions exclusively within your private enterprise cloud (AWS, Azure, or GCP VPC) or on-premise infrastructure. All API agreements enforce Zero Data Retention (ZDR), meaning your data is never stored by third parties or utilized to train public AI models.

Can the AI Content Generator capture our exact brand tone and style guide?

Yes. We fine-tune models using Low-Rank Adaptation (LoRA) and custom multi-shot prompt templates derived directly from your published content, style guide, and brand manual. Every generated output passes through an automated tone-compliance evaluator before publishing.

What document formats can the Virtual Research Assistant ingest and cite?

Our research assistant supports complex multi-format document ingestion including scanned and native PDFs, Word documents, Excel spreadsheets, PowerPoint presentations, SEC EDGAR filings, and SQL databases. It extracts tables and charts with high fidelity and provides clickable inline citations linking back to the exact source page and paragraph.

What is the typical timeline and cost to develop a custom Generative AI solution?

A production-ready Minimum Viable Product (MVP) or prototype typically ships within 2 to 4 weeks. Full enterprise deployments with custom fine-tuning, complex API integrations, and red-team security audits generally range from 6 to 12 weeks. We offer flexible engagement models: dedicated AI engineer pods, milestone-based fixed pricing, or hybrid advisory sprints.
