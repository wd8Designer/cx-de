Operational Architecture

The Dual-Delivery Engineering Advantage

How Cypherox eliminates the friction of traditional outsourcing, combining local European strategic accountability with high-velocity global engineering pods.

TRADITIONAL OUTSOURCING

Fragmented Agency Model

  • High Developer Churn: Projects handed to shifting junior freelancers with high turnover and zero retained domain knowledge.
  • Management Black Box: Account managers act as intermediaries, filtering technical realities and slowing feedback cycles.
  • Minimal Timezone Overlap: Engineers log off before European workdays start, causing 24-hour delays for single pull requests.
  • Regulatory Risk & IP Gray Zones: Unverified compliance with GDPR (DSGVO), uncertain code ownership, and insecure development laptops.
THE CYPHEROX POD MODEL

Cohesive Dedicated Engineering

  • 100% In-House Senior Pods: Permanent full-time engineers with 6+ years average experience, dedicated exclusively to your roadmap.
  • Direct Engineering Collaboration: Developers work inside your Slack, Jira, and GitHub with daily standups and transparent sprint velocity.
  • 4 to 6 Hours Daily CET Overlap: Real-time pair programming, live code reviews, and instant unblocking during core business hours.
  • European Law & ISO 27001 Protection: Strict contractual IP transfer under German/UK jurisdiction, NDA protection, and SOC 2 security controls.
