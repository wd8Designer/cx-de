How it works

The process to hire dedicated developers

Four steps from first call to a developer working on your project.

  1. 01

    Initial consultation

    Fill in the form for a free consultation and talk your project through with our team: the technical requirement, the scope, the stack and the timeline.

  2. 02

    Resource matching

    We identify the developers in our team who fit that requirement. You review and interview them, and you pick the ones you want.

  3. 03

    Onboarding

    We handle the contract, the paperwork and the communication channels, and make sure the developers have the access and the context to start.

  4. 04

    Project management and support

    Ongoing management against your goals and deadlines, with regular performance reviews and your feedback fed back into the work.

Ask our team

Can I hire dedicated developers without signing a long-term contract?

Yes. You can hire dedicated developers for exactly as long as the project needs them, with no long-term commitment and no long-term contract to sign.

Why should I hire dedicated resources?

You save the time it takes to train an employee and you get people who are already skilled in the work. Our developers have years of experience behind them, so the project starts moving without a ramp-up period first.

Do you sign an NDA while we hire a dedicated development team?

Yes. Data security is a first-order concern here, so we sign a Non-Disclosure Agreement before the work starts and your sensitive information stays covered by it.

Can we track our project?

Yes. We keep clients updated on a set rhythm, work through the project management and communication tools you already use, and report on progress in detail rather than in summary.
