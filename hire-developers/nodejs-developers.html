Questions Before You Hire

The questions teams usually raise before adding Node engineers.

Which Node frameworks do your developers use?

Express and NestJS most often, along with others that fit the project. We choose based on the system's needs rather than defaulting to one framework.

Is Node.js a good fit for our project?

It excels at I/O-heavy and real-time work like APIs, chat and streaming. For CPU-intensive processing it is less ideal, and we will tell you honestly if your case points elsewhere.

Can I engage Node developers in Germany flexibly?

Yes. Full time, hourly or fixed cost, set around the scope and length of your project.

Can Node developers build real-time features?

Yes. Node's handling of concurrent connections makes it well suited to chat, live notifications and other real-time functionality, which our developers build regularly.

What drives the cost of hiring Node developers?

Seniority, the hiring model and the engagement's size. Send the brief and we will outline a fit.
