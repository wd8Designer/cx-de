Questions Before You Hire

What teams usually want to clear up before adding front-end engineers.

Which frameworks do your front-end developers work in?

React, Angular and Vue most often, along with the surrounding tooling. We match developers to the framework your project already runs on rather than pushing a preference.

Can a front-end developer work from our existing designs?

Yes. Taking design files and building them into responsive, accessible interfaces is core front-end work, and our developers handle the detail and interaction states that designs imply.

Can I engage front-end developers in Germany flexibly?

Yes. Full time, hourly or fixed cost, set around the scope and length of your project.

Do your front-end developers handle accessibility and performance?

Yes. Both are treated as part of the build rather than later fixes, covering responsive behaviour, load speed and accessibility standards.

What drives the cost of hiring front-end developers?

Seniority, the hiring model and the engagement's size. Send the brief and we will outline something that fits.
