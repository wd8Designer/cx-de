Questions Before You Hire

What teams usually want to clear up before adding full stack developers.

When does a full stack developer make more sense than specialists?

When features benefit from single ownership and handoffs slow you down, common in MVPs, lean teams and smaller products. For very large or deeply specialised work, dedicated front-end and back-end developers may fit better.

Which stacks do your full stack developers work in?

Common combinations across JavaScript, Python, PHP and their frameworks, matched to what your product uses. We align the developer to your existing stack rather than imposing one.

Can I engage full stack developers in Germany flexibly?

Yes. Full time, hourly or fixed cost, set around the scope and length of your project.

Can a full stack developer build an MVP solo?

Often, yes. For a focused MVP, one capable full stack developer can cover the whole build, which is frequently the fastest route from idea to working product.

What drives the cost of hiring full stack developers?

Seniority, the hiring model and the engagement's size. Send the brief and we will outline a fit.
