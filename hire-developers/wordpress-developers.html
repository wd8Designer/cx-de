Questions Before You Hire

What teams usually want to clear up before adding WordPress engineers.

Can your developers build a custom theme from our design?

Yes. Turning a design into a bespoke WordPress theme, rather than forcing it into a pre-made one, is standard work and gives you a site that matches your brand exactly.

Can you fix a slow or insecure WordPress site?

Yes. Auditing a site, removing problematic plugins, hardening security and improving load speed is common work on sites that have grown unwieldy over time.

Can I engage WordPress developers in Germany flexibly?

Yes. Full time, hourly or fixed cost, set around the scope and length of your project.

Do you build custom plugins?

Yes. Where the functionality you need isn't well served by existing plugins, our developers build custom ones tailored to your site and kept maintainable.

What drives the cost of hiring WordPress developers?

Seniority, the hiring model and the engagement's size. Send the brief and we will outline a fit.
