Questions Before You Hire

What teams usually want settled before adding Laravel engineers.

Can Laravel developers build an API-only backend?

Yes. Laravel works well as a pure API backend for a separate front end or mobile app, and our developers build it that way when your architecture calls for it.

Can your developers take over an existing Laravel project?

Yes. Inheriting a Laravel codebase, understanding its structure and continuing the build is a common way these engagements start.

Can I engage Laravel developers in Germany flexibly?

Yes. Full time, hourly or fixed cost, set around the scope and length of your project.

Do your developers use Laravel's ecosystem tools?

Yes. Where they fit the project, tools like Horizon, Sanctum and the broader ecosystem are used to save build time rather than reimplementing their functionality.

What drives the cost of hiring Laravel developers?

Seniority, the hiring model and the engagement's size. Send the brief and we will outline a fit.
