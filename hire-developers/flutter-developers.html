Questions Before You Hire

What teams usually want to know before committing to Flutter.

Will a Flutter app really feel native?

A well-built one does. Flutter renders its own UI consistently across platforms, and our developers reach for native code on the features where it matters, so the result holds up next to native apps.

Is Flutter right for every app?

Not always. It suits most product and content apps well; very hardware-heavy or platform-specific apps sometimes argue for native. We will be honest if your case leans that way.

Can I engage Flutter developers in Germany flexibly?

Yes. Full time, hourly or fixed cost, set around the scope and length of your build.

Can your developers take over an existing Flutter project?

Yes. Picking up a Dart codebase mid-way, understanding its structure and carrying it forward is a common way these engagements begin.
