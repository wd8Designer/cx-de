Questions Before You Hire

What teams usually want to clear up before adding e-commerce engineers.

Which e-commerce platforms do your developers work with?

Shopify, WooCommerce, Magento, BigCommerce and others. We match developers to the platform your store already runs on rather than pushing a particular one.

Can a developer migrate our store to another platform?

Yes. Moving a store between platforms, including products, customers and order history, is work our developers handle with care to avoid disrupting live sales.

Can I engage e-commerce developers in Germany flexibly?

Yes. Full time, hourly or fixed cost, set around the scope and length of your project, including seasonal peaks.

Can your developers connect our store to other systems?

Yes. Integrating the store with inventory, shipping, ERP and marketing tools is common work, so your store runs as part of one connected operation.

What drives the cost of hiring e-commerce developers?

Platform, seniority, the hiring model and the engagement's size. Send the brief and we will outline a fit.
