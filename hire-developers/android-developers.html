Questions Before You Hire

What teams tend to ask before adding Android engineers.

Kotlin or Java?

New work is written in Kotlin. Where you have an existing Java app, our developers maintain and extend it, introducing Kotlin where that serves the project.

How do you handle the device fragmentation problem?

By testing against the device and OS range that matters to your users and writing code that degrades gracefully, rather than targeting only the newest hardware.

Can I engage Android developers in Germany flexibly?

Yes. Full time, hourly or fixed cost, set around the length and scope of your work.

Will the developer publish to Google Play?

Yes. That covers preparing the release, managing the Play process and shipping later updates, not just the build itself.

What drives the cost of hiring Android developers?

Seniority, your chosen model and the engagement's size. Send the brief and we will lay out an arrangement that fits.
