Questions Before You Hire

What teams usually want to clear up before adding graphic designers.

Will the designer follow our existing brand guidelines?

Yes. Working within an established brand system is the norm; our designers learn your guidelines and produce assets that stay true to them rather than imposing a new look.

We don't have brand guidelines yet. Can you build them?

Yes. Developing a logo, colour palette and visual system from scratch is part of what our designers do, and it gives later work a consistent foundation.

Can I engage graphic designers in Germany flexibly?

Yes. Full time, hourly or fixed cost, set around your creative workload and the length of the work.

What formats can your designers deliver?

Print, digital, social and marketing collateral, supplied in the file formats your team and vendors need to use them.

What drives the cost of hiring graphic designers?

Experience, the hiring model and the volume of work. Send the brief and we will outline something that fits.
