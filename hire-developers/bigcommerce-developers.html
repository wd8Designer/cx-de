Questions Before You Hire

What teams usually want to clear up before adding BigCommerce engineers.

Can your developers build a headless BigCommerce store?

Yes. Using BigCommerce as a commerce backend behind a custom front end is work our developers handle when you want full control over the storefront experience.

Can you customise a BigCommerce theme?

Yes. Working in Stencil lets our developers tailor themes well beyond the built-in settings, so the store matches your brand rather than a default layout.

Can I engage BigCommerce developers in Germany flexibly?

Yes. Full time, hourly or fixed cost, set around the scope and length of your project, including seasonal peaks.

Can you migrate our store to BigCommerce?

Yes. Moving products, customers and order history onto BigCommerce is handled carefully to keep the live store running throughout.

What drives the cost of hiring BigCommerce developers?

Seniority, the hiring model and the engagement's size. Headless builds typically call for more. Send the brief and we will outline a fit.
