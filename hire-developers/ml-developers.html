Questions Before You Hire

What teams usually want to clear up before adding ML engineers.

Do we have enough data for machine learning?

It depends on the problem. Our developers will assess your data honestly early on, and tell you if the quantity or quality isn't yet there rather than building a model that can't perform.

What's the difference between hiring ML and AI developers?

ML developers focus on building, training and deploying models from your data. AI developers more often integrate existing models into product features. Which you need depends on whether the model itself is the work.

Can I engage ML developers in Germany flexibly?

Yes. Full time, hourly or fixed cost, set around the scope and length of your project.

Can your developers deploy a model into our product?

Yes. Taking a trained model into production and setting up monitoring so it keeps performing is part of the lifecycle our developers cover.

What drives the cost of hiring ML developers?

Seniority, the complexity of the problem, the hiring model and the engagement's size. Send the brief and we will outline a fit.
