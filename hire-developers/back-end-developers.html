Questions Before You Hire

What teams usually want to clear up before adding back-end engineers.

Which back-end stacks do your developers work in?

Node, Python, PHP and their major frameworks, among others. We match developers to the stack your system already runs rather than steering you toward a particular one.

Can a back-end developer work with our existing front end?

Yes. Building APIs and services that your current front end or mobile apps consume is standard work, and our developers fit into an existing architecture rather than requiring a rebuild.

Can I engage back-end developers in Germany flexibly?

Yes. Full time, hourly or fixed cost, set around the scope and length of your project.

Do your developers handle security and scaling?

Yes. Both are treated as part of the design: securing the system against common risks and structuring it to handle growth in traffic and data.

What drives the cost of hiring back-end developers?

Seniority, the hiring model and the engagement's size. Send the brief and we will outline something that fits.
