Article 02

Engagement Models and Statements of Work (SOW)

Cypherox provides technical services under several flexible engagement structures, each governed by an associated Statement of Work, work authorization, or sprint backlog:

  • Dedicated Engineering Pods / Staff Augmentation: Senior developers, architects, QA engineers, or data scientists dedicated to Client's roadmap on a monthly or hourly basis, working under Client's day-to-day technical guidance with 40 hours per week full-time allocation.
  • Turnkey / Fixed-Price Milestone Builds: Defined software scopes with specific acceptance criteria, project milestones, deliverable deadlines, and fixed billing schedules.
  • Time & Materials (T&M): Agile delivery billed according to approved timesheets and hourly/daily rates, ideal for evolving roadmaps, discovery research, and generative AI prototyping.
  • Ongoing Maintenance & DevOps SLA: Scheduled monthly retainer agreements covering infrastructure monitoring, security updates, bug fixes, and continuous performance tuning.

Any changes, expansions, or alterations to the agreed project scope must be documented in a written Change Order signed by authorized representatives of both parties before implementation.

Article 03

Intellectual Property Rights and Code Ownership

Cypherox operates with a strict, client-centric intellectual property policy designed to guarantee full corporate ownership of your custom assets.

3.1 Custom Deliverables & Work for Hire

Upon receipt of full and final payment for the applicable invoices, all bespoke source code, architectural designs, application binaries, database schemas, algorithms, and documentation developed specifically for Client under the relevant SOW shall be considered "Work Made for Hire" and shall become the exclusive intellectual property of the Client.

3.2 Pre-Existing IP and Open Source Components

Cypherox retains all ownership rights in its pre-existing proprietary libraries, developer toolkits, starter scaffolding, and reusable utilities ("Background IP"). To the extent that any Background IP is incorporated into the Deliverables, Cypherox grants Client a perpetual, irrevocable, worldwide, royalty-free, non-exclusive license to use, modify, and deploy such Background IP solely in conjunction with the Deliverables.

Our 100% IP Guarantee

We never retain hidden claims over your product logic. All git repositories, continuous integration pipelines, and cloud infrastructure configurations deployed under your project are transferred to your administrative control with complete transparency.

3.3 Open Source Software (OSS) Compliance

Cypherox only utilizes standard, business-friendly open source packages (e.g., MIT, Apache 2.0, BSD) in production builds. We strictly avoid copyleft licenses (such as GPLv3) in proprietary commercial software unless specifically requested and approved by Client in writing.

Article 04

Enterprise Artificial Intelligence, LLMs & Data Ethics

For projects involving Generative AI, Large Language Models (LLMs), machine learning pipelines, or autonomous agent systems, the following specific terms apply:

  • Client Training Data: Client retains full ownership, title, and intellectual property rights over all proprietary datasets, documents, and prompt inputs supplied to Cypherox for model fine-tuning, RAG indexing, or embedding generation.
  • No Unauthorized Model Training: Cypherox covenants that Client proprietary data will never be used to train public, open, or third-party foundation models without Client's explicit prior written authorization.
  • Third-Party Model Providers: Where solutions interface with external LLM APIs (e.g., OpenAI, Anthropic, Google Cloud Vertex AI, AWS Bedrock), Client acknowledges that such services are governed by the respective cloud provider's terms of service and acceptable use policies.
  • Deterministic Guardrails & Validation: While Cypherox engineers comprehensive system guardrails, grounding mechanisms, and hallucination safeguards, Client acknowledges that probabilistic AI models inherently require human-in-the-loop review for critical compliance, medical, legal, or financial decisions.
