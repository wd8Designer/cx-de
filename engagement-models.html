Ask Your Question

Can we switch engagement models after the project has started?

Yes. One practical path is a fixed cost first release, followed by a dedicated team or time and material once the roadmap opens up. We make the switch at a milestone or sprint boundary, so billing, ownership and handover are agreed before anything changes.

How do you price a project when the scope is still unclear?

We avoid forcing a fixed price onto unclear scope. A short, effort-based discovery phase lets engineers test assumptions, prototype risky parts such as AI model behavior and document requirements. Once scope settles, the remaining work can move to a fixed quote.

How much control do we keep over a dedicated engineering team?

You set sprint priorities and work with the team in your own tools, such as Jira, Slack or Microsoft Teams. Cypherox handles hiring, employment and people management, so your leads direct the engineering work without carrying the overhead of a larger in-house team.

Which engagement model suits AI agent or generative AI development?

It depends on how proven the use case is. Early agent work, where evaluation results shape the design, fits time and material. Once model behavior, integrations and guardrails are documented, a fixed cost build works. Production AI systems that need ongoing tuning suit a dedicated team.
