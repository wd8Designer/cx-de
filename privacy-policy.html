Article 01

Introduction & Data Controller Identity

This Privacy Policy applies to personal data collected and processed by Cypherox Technologies ("Cypherox", "we", "us", or "our") when you visit our website (cypherox.com), engage our software engineering and artificial intelligence teams, hire dedicated developer pods, communicate with our sales representatives, or participate in discovery consultations.

Under the European Union General Data Protection Regulation ("EU GDPR"), the UK Data Protection Act 2018 ("UK GDPR"), and applicable international privacy statutes, the primary Data Controller responsible for your personal data is Cypherox Technologies.

Controller vs. Processor Distinction

When you visit our public website or submit an inquiry, Cypherox acts as a Data Controller. When our engineers process proprietary client datasets, database records, or end-user records under an executed Statement of Work (SOW), Cypherox operates strictly as a Data Processor on behalf of the Client under an executed Data Processing Addendum (DPA).

Article 02

Information We Collect

We collect information directly from you, automatically through your website interactions, and through commercial communications:

2.1 Information You Provide Voluntarily

  • Contact & Identification Data: Full name, corporate email address, phone number, company name, job title, and geographic jurisdiction provided when booking a discovery session, submitting a project inquiry, or requesting developer resumes.
  • Project & Technical Requirements: Technical briefs, architecture specifications, tech stack preferences, feature roadmaps, and scheduling availability provided during project scoping calls.

2.2 Information Collected Automatically

  • Technical & Device Identifiers: IP addresses, browser engine and version, operating system, referring URLs, time-zone settings, and approximate geographic location.
  • Usage & Interaction Data: Pages visited, time spent on specific case studies or service offerings, scroll depth, and interaction with navigation mega-menus.
Article 03

How We Use Your Information

We utilize collected data solely for legitimate commercial and engineering purposes:

  • Service Delivery & Staffing: Matching specialized senior engineers, full-stack developers, and AI architects to your exact project scope and technical requirements.
  • Communication & Scheduling: Coordinating technical discovery calls, replying to project inquiries, and delivering sprint progress reports.
  • Contracting & Invoicing: Preparing Statements of Work (SOW), processing milestone billing, and maintaining accounting compliance.
  • Security & Threat Mitigation: Monitoring website infrastructure against malicious attacks, DDoS attempts, and unauthorized access.
  • Platform Optimization: Analyzing site navigation patterns to enhance speed, layout clarity, and user experience across desktop and mobile devices.
Article 04

Enterprise AI, Model Training & Proprietary Datasets

As an enterprise Generative AI and autonomous agent engineering firm, Cypherox upholds stringent confidentiality and privacy covenants regarding artificial intelligence systems:

Zero Model Leakage & No Public Model Training

We guarantee that Client proprietary data, source code, document embeddings, and prompt inputs are NEVER used to train, fine-tune, or improve public or foundation LLMs (such as OpenAI, Anthropic, Google, or Meta). All data processed within client AI solutions remains completely isolated in dedicated private virtual private clouds (VPCs) or containerized enterprise environments.

4.1 Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) Security

Vector databases and semantic knowledge indexes built for Client applications utilize strict role-based access controls (RBAC), tenant-level isolation, and encryption at rest (AES-256) and in transit (TLS 1.3).

4.2 Data Sanitization & PII Scrubbing

Where training pipelines or synthetic data generation are commissioned, our engineers apply automated Personally Identifiable Information (PII) masking and token redaction routines before embedding or vectorization.

Article 05

Legal Bases for Processing Under GDPR

For individuals residing within the European Economic Area (EEA) and the United Kingdom, we process personal data under the following lawful bases:

  • Contractual Necessity (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR): Processing required to negotiate, execute, and fulfill our engineering agreements, developer onboarding, and Statement of Work deliverables.
  • Legitimate Interests (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR): Managing our commercial relationship, safeguarding our digital infrastructure, preventing fraud, and optimizing business operations.
  • Consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR): Where you have provided unambiguous consent, such as opting into email technical newsletters or non-essential analytics cookies.
  • Legal Obligation (Art. 6(1)(c) GDPR): Maintaining statutory financial records, tax disclosures, and corporate audit compliance.
Article 06

Cookies & Tracking Technologies

We utilize cookies and similar technologies to facilitate essential website operations, enhance navigation, and analyze aggregated audience trends:

  • Strictly Necessary Cookies: Required for fundamental site functions, such as security headers, session management, and mobile menu state retention.
  • Performance & Analytics Cookies: Aggregated, anonymized telemetry (such as Google Analytics with IP anonymization) measuring page load times, popular tech stack pages, and visitor flow.
  • Preference Cookies: Storing user layout choices, such as accordion states and cookie consent preferences.

You can control or disable cookies via your browser settings at any time. Disabling strictly necessary cookies may affect navigation functionality.

Article 07

Data Sharing & Third-Party Sub-Processors

We do not sell, rent, monetize, or trade your personal data. We only share information with vetted third-party service providers who assist in our technical and business operations under strict Data Processing Agreements:

  • Cloud & Infrastructure Providers: Amazon Web Services (AWS), Google Cloud Platform (GCP), and Microsoft Azure for enterprise hosting, encrypted storage, and staging build environments.
  • Collaboration & DevOps Platforms: GitHub, GitLab, Jira, and Slack utilized for sprint management, issue tracking, and direct developer collaboration.
  • Communication & Scheduling Tools: Calendly and corporate email servers for scheduling discovery sessions and technical consultations.
  • Legal & Professional Advisors: Certified public accountants, external auditors, and legal counsel operating under statutory confidentiality duties.
Article 08

International Cross-Border Data Transfers

Cypherox operates global engineering and consulting facilities across India, the United Kingdom, and the United States. Consequently, personal data may be transferred to, stored, and processed in jurisdictions outside your country of residence.

When transferring data originating in the EEA or UK to jurisdictions without an adequacy decision, Cypherox implements robust cross-border safeguard mechanisms:

  • Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs): Incorporating the European Commission's approved standard contractual clauses and the UK International Data Transfer Addendum into our contracts.
  • Technical Safeguards: Enforcing end-to-end encryption in transit (TLS 1.3), encryption at rest (AES-256), strict credential vaulting, and multi-factor authentication across all cross-border engineering links.
Article 09

Data Retention & Erasure Policies

We retain personal data only for as long as necessary to satisfy the purposes outlined in this Privacy Policy, unless a longer retention period is mandated by law (e.g., tax, audit, or statutory compliance obligations):

  • Project Inquiries & Discovery Notes: Retained for twenty-four (24) months to facilitate ongoing technical scoping discussions, after which they are archived or securely deleted.
  • Active Client Contract Records: Retained for the duration of the commercial engagement plus seven (7) years post-termination to comply with corporate tax and audit statutes.
  • Staging & Build Artifacts: Staging environments, test databases, and developer temporary snapshots are permanently purged within thirty (30) days following final production deployment and sign-off.
Article 10

Cybersecurity Safeguards & Operational Controls

Cypherox maintains comprehensive physical, organizational, and technical cybersecurity safeguards designed to protect data against accidental loss, unauthorized destruction, alteration, or disclosure:

  • Encryption: AES-256 encryption for all data at rest and TLS 1.3 encryption for all data in transit across public and private networks.
  • Endpoint Security: Managed developer hardware with full-disk encryption, centralized anti-malware monitoring, and automated security patch management.
  • Network & Access Isolation: Zero-Trust Network Architecture, VPN-gated private repos, least-privilege access policies, and biometric physical facility controls at our engineering centers.
  • Continuous Vulnerability Scanning: Automated static application security testing (SAST) and dependency vulnerability monitoring built directly into our CI/CD pipelines.

Depending on your location, you possess defined statutory rights concerning your personal data:

Statutory Right Description SLA Turnaround
Right of Access Request a copy of personal data we hold about you in a structured, portable format. Within 30 Days
Right to Rectification Request correction of inaccurate or incomplete personal information. Within 14 Days
Right to Erasure Request permanent deletion of your personal data ("Right to be Forgotten"). Within 30 Days
Right to Restrict Processing Request that we limit processing of your data while a dispute is resolved. Within 14 Days
