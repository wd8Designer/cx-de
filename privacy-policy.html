Recipients of your data

We do not sell your personal data. We share it only in these cases:

  • Service providers (processors): cloud hosting, payment, project management and analytics providers that process data for us under a data processing agreement (Art. 28 GDPR)
  • Professional advisers: lawyers, accountants and auditors, bound by professional confidentiality
  • Subcontractors: specialist developers and designers who help deliver projects and are bound by confidentiality and data protection duties
  • Authorities: where the law, a court order or a lawful official request requires it
  • Business transfers: in a merger, acquisition or sale of assets, with equivalent protection

Transfers outside the EU

We are based outside the EU, and our team and systems are in India. Your data is therefore processed in India and, through some providers, in other countries. India has no EU adequacy decision.

Where data is transferred to us or onward to a third country, we protect it with one of these safeguards under Chapter V GDPR:

  • the EU Standard Contractual Clauses (Commission Implementing Decision (EU) 2021/914), together with a transfer impact assessment and supplementary measures where needed
  • for US providers, certification under the EU-US Data Privacy Framework
  • in limited cases, your explicit consent, after we inform you of the risks

You can request a copy of the safeguards by writing to info@cypherox.de.

Your rights

Under the GDPR you have these rights:

  • Access (Art. 15): receive a copy of your personal data and information about how we use it
  • Rectification (Art. 16): have inaccurate or incomplete data corrected
  • Erasure (Art. 17): have your data deleted where we no longer need it or you withdraw consent, unless a legal retention duty applies
  • Restriction (Art. 18): have processing paused in certain cases, for example while you contest accuracy
  • Data portability (Art. 20): receive your data in a structured, machine-readable format where processing is based on consent or a contract
  • Objection (Art. 21): object at any time to processing based on legitimate interests, and to direct marketing. For direct marketing, we stop at once
  • Withdrawal of consent (Art. 7(3)): withdraw consent at any time, with effect for the future
  • No solely automated decisions (Art. 22): see section 6

To use your rights, email info@cypherox.de. We reply within one month. In complex cases we may extend this by up to two further months and will tell you why. We may need to verify your identity first. Requests are free, unless they are manifestly unfounded or excessive.

Cookies and similar technologies

We use cookies and similar technologies only as the German Telecommunications Digital Services Data Protection Act (TDDDG) and the GDPR allow. A cookie is a small file stored on your device.

Cookie type Purpose Consent required?
Strictly necessary Security, form submission, session management, saving your cookie choice No
Analytics Understand how visitors use our website, for example Google Analytics Yes
Functional Remember your preferences Yes
Marketing Remarketing and targeted advertising Yes

When you first visit our website, a consent banner lets you accept all, reject all or choose by category. Rejecting is as easy as accepting. We load non-essential cookies only after you consent. You can change or withdraw your choice at any time through the cookie settings link in the website footer. We record your choice as proof of consent.

You can also block cookies in your browser settings. Blocking strictly necessary cookies may limit what the website can do.

Marketing communications

We send marketing emails and newsletters only with your consent, confirmed by a double opt-in, or as § 7(3) UWG allows for existing customers: we received your address in connection with a sale, we advertise our own similar services, and you have not objected.

Every marketing email contains an unsubscribe link. You can also opt out by:

  • clicking "unsubscribe" in any marketing email
  • emailing info@cypherox.de with the subject "Unsubscribe"

You can opt out at any time, without costs other than transmission costs at basic rates. Opting out does not stop service-related messages about your project or contract.

Data security

We protect your data with appropriate technical and organisational measures (Art. 32 GDPR) against unauthorised access, loss, alteration and disclosure. These include:

  • encryption of data in transit (TLS/SSL) and at rest where appropriate
  • access controls and role-based permissions
  • regular security assessments and code reviews
  • staff training on data protection and information security
  • secure deletion of data we no longer need

If a personal data breach is likely to put people's rights at risk, we notify the competent supervisory authority within 72 hours of becoming aware of it (Art. 33 GDPR). If the risk is high, we inform the affected individuals without undue delay (Art. 34 GDPR).

Third-party links

Our website may link to third-party sites, such as partner platforms, review sites and social networks. This policy does not cover them. We are not responsible for their content or privacy practices, so please read their privacy policies.

Children

Our website and services are aimed at business professionals and not at anyone under 18. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children. If we learn that we have, we will delete it promptly. If you think we hold a minor's data, email info@cypherox.de.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy to reflect changes in our practices, the law or regulatory guidance. We publish the new version on our website with an updated date. For material changes, we notify active clients and newsletter subscribers by email at least 14 days before they take effect.

Right to lodge a complaint

Please contact us first at info@cypherox.de. We investigate and reply within one month.

You also have the right to complain to a data protection supervisory authority (Art. 77 GDPR). You can choose the authority in the EU member state where you live or work, or where the alleged infringement took place. In Germany, these are the Federal Commissioner for Data Protection and Freedom of Information BfDI and the data protection authorities of the federal states.
